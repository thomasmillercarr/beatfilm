# Slop: the defaults that make a film look generated

Credit: the never-ship list and the default reaches are adapted (condensed and partly rewritten) from
motionmaxxing's `references/slop.md` and `judgment.md` (Apache-2.0, github.com/Tejashmakwana/motionmaxxing). This file is under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt). See NOTICE.

None of these is banned as an idea. Each is wrong when you arrive at it by default rather than
choosing it for a reason you can say in one sentence about this film.

## Never ship (delete it; do not justify it)

1. **Page chrome**: the film's or brand's name in a corner, scene numbers ("01 / 04"), timecodes,
   bpm or resolution text, progress bars, corner brackets, tracked-caps labels over shots.
2. **Deck layouts**: a held headline block on the left with a device on the right, a kicker above
   a headline, title-then-content frames.
3. **Generic UI**: a rounded card with a status dot and "label: value", "Good morning" cards, grey
   skeleton bars, invented app chrome where the subject's real UI exists.
4. **Invented proof**: stat walls, counters to unsourced numbers, fake testimonials, on-screen disclaimers.
5. **Slogan copy**: "Not just X. It's Y.", one-word triplets, "Unlock", "Meet X", "the future of".
6. **Flat colour swatches per scene**, or a small card floating on an empty ground.
7. **A web-footer ending**: logo + tagline + button + URL, or any end card held past 1.4 s.
8. **Crossfades between scenes** (see transitions.md).
9. **A film nobody looked at.** No render is "done" until the stills sheet has been opened and checked.

## The default reaches (check the plan for these before writing code)

| Reach | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Making a page, not staging a picture | Would this frame work as a slide or a website hero? Then it is a page. |
| Showing everything | What is the one true thing? What did I leave out on purpose? |
| States, not events | Does the plan say what happens and what causes it, or only what is shown? |
| Premium pastiche (near-black, glow, one gold accent, a serif-italic word) | Is the ground dark because the brand is dark, or because dark feels expensive? |
| The name's first association (stars, rockets, shields) | What was my first idea, and did I look past it? |
| Effects standing in for quality: a new technique every beat | Does the idea need this effect, or is it showing effort? |
| A script with illustrations | Does the picture have its own job, or does it illustrate each noun? |
| Minimalism as a hiding place: something small on a void, long holds | Is more than a third of the film nearly still? Where is the frame someone would screenshot? |
| The house style: a giant cropped word, texture in letters, a colour-flood wipe, a dot that becomes the logo | Did this film choose it, or is it the film I always make? |

## The fixes that matter most, in order

1. Remove the page (chrome, deck layouts, swatches, footer ending).
2. Replace crossfades with cuts on motion that carry an object.
3. Give each role its own easing: entrances fast out then a long tail, exits accelerate, counters
   step, drift is linear. One global ease is the loudest generated-film tell.
4. Kill dead frames. Holds only after a payoff (0.5-1 s) or as a stated breath.
5. Make type behave like a person set it: words arrive whole on the rhythm, hierarchy by scale not
   bold, the key word acts out its meaning, surviving words never re-centre.
6. Spend colour and effects once: one colour for "acting", one for "done", no glow as wallpaper.
