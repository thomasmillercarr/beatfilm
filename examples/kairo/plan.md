# Kairo launch film: plan

**Brief:** "Kairo is a calendar app that plans your week for you: it schedules deep work, moves meetings
and declines what doesn't fit. Launching early access. Make a 30 s launch film."

- **Logline:** your week, rebuilt.
- **Motif:** calendar blocks. Chaos (grey/violet, overlapping, shaking) → the same grid assembling itself
  in order (orange focus, violet meetings) → pulled back as a finished week behind the lockup.
- **Music:** "Inspired", Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0), 120 bpm, steady grid. Cut 73.53 s + 31 s = 15 bars + 1 s
  tail. Phrase energy: 0.58 (bars 0–3) → **0.94 drop at bar 4** → 0.77 dip (bars 8–11) → 0.93 finale.
  Hits at 2.0 s and 16.0 s.
- **Look:** ink #121117 for the problem, paper #f2eee5 for the solution, accent #ff5b23, violet #7b6cff.
  Bricolage Grotesque (display/UI), Newsreader italic (emotional words), JetBrains Mono (data).
- **Format:** 1920×1080 page, 30 fps → 3840×2160.
